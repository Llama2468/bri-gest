Endocrinology 2026 so far: the high-yield evidence

RCTs, meta-analyses and guidelines from top-tier general and endocrine journals, indexed in PubMed from 1 January to 26 November 2026. This is a pilot: the top tier is covered completely, but the specialty journals contribute guidelines only.

This page is not a Bri-gest digest. The Bri-gest digests show abstracts verbatim with no AI content. Here, classifications, take-homes and scores were drafted with AI assistance and spot-checked by the author. Result lines are as reported in each abstract, and the full abstract is one click away on PubMed. Treat take-homes as editorial, not as the source.
